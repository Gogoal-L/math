## F049: stabilization is not an Eth transition theorem

Original source: [body](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/A-stable-coordinate-that-is-not-a-coordinate-in-four-variables-October-5-2026/build/source/sections), [Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/docs/049.md).

1. Present \`A=k[p,s,u,F,J]/(H)\`, with \`x=s²-u²+pF\` and \`H=x²F-(1+2sx)J-pJ²-u\`; the determinant-one polynomial coordinate change makes \`A\` a polynomial ring of four variables and \`p\` the explicit fifth-degree expression in the main theorem.
2. Prove stabilization: the explicit locally nilpotent derivation \`Delta\` obeys \`Delta(H)=p\`. Its exponential after adjoining **one independent** \`w\` maps \`H\` to \`H+pw\`; a unit-determinant polynomial substitution then eliminates the relation with \`p\` left as a coordinate.
3. Prove noncoordinateness in original dimension: assume \`p\` is a coordinate, use a weighted filtration (negative weights essential), obtain a nonzero locally nilpotent derivation of \`B=k[x,y,z,u]/(xy-z(z+1))\` fixing a nonzero element of ideal \`I\`.
4. Pull back \`I\` to \`C=k[a,d,b,c,u]/(ac-bd-1)\` where it becomes principal \`(v)\`, \`v=a³b+a²u²-d²\`; lift the additive action preserving fiber weights. Grading isolates \`a²u²-d²=(au-d)(au+d)\`, forcing \`a,d,u\` invariant; the remaining nonzero coefficient must lie in \`k[a,d,u]\` but carry fiber weight \`-2\`, impossible.

Independent symbolic checks using SymPy: \`xy-z(z+1)=p(H+u)\`, \`Delta(x)=Delta(y)=Delta(z)=0\`, \`Delta(H)=p\` and the determinant-one matrix transformation all simplified identically to zero error. These five algebraic spot checks do not verify the full LND/line-bundle/rigidity proof.

**DPT translation:** an auxiliary lifted or overparameterized model can be computationally useful without establishing a physically admissible/identifiable coordinate system for the original DAE. This is an **analogy of logical distinction**. It does not imply Eth requires an auxiliary state, does not prohibit its implicit consistency lift, and provides no DPT reset formula. **Priority: WATCH / conceptual boundary**, not a first-line Sol9 theorem.

## F149: inward-flux proof rather than generic convexity

Original source: [body](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Uniform-Permanence-in-Weakly-Reversible-Mass-Action-Systems-October-5-2026/build/sections), [source figure](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Uniform-Permanence-in-Weakly-Reversible-Mass-Action-Systems-October-5-2026/build/figures/intervals.tex), [Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/docs/149.md).

1. Weak reversibility means every reaction has a directed return path. The mass-action vector field is \`f(x)=sum_{y->y'} k x^y (y'-y)\`, confined to one stoichiometric affine class \`P=(c+S)∩R^d_{>0}\` at fixed positive reaction rates.
2. An induction on dimension builds a finite family \`F_h(x)=min_lambda(r_lambda·x+b_lambda(h))\`, where an **active** slope approximates the exponent vector \`p=log x/log h\` even under an arbitrary positive, possibly discontinuous tolerance. Local families at minimum exponents \`t_j\` are combined by shifts \`-sum_{l<j}h^{q_l}\`. The visual interval schematic shows each closed cut interval \`[q_{j-1},q_j]\` lies within its own open validity neighborhood, including cut endpoints.
3. For an active slope \`r\`, order reaction complexes by \`r·y\`. Decompose \`r·f(x)\` into upward-minus-downward fluxes \`Q_j(x)\` over level cuts. A downward edge has a return path with an upward crossing; monomial dominance of the latter (for sufficiently small \`h\`) makes \`Q_j>=0\`, strictly positive for crossed cuts. This **special reaction-graph mechanism** yields positive derivative for active affine components not orthogonal to the stoichiometric subspace.
4. The plateau \`K_h={F_h=F_h(c)}\` is nonempty compact convex with \`K_h ⊂ int B_{sqrt h}\`; the right derivative of the finite minimum is the **minimum over active** directional derivatives. Strict increase on the class outside \`K_h\` forces finite-time entry; forward invariance follows from monotonicity and \`F_h≤F_h(c)\`.
5. Every initial positive state enters its own \`K_{h_0}\`; successive \`h_{j+1}=min(sqrt(h_j),h_P)\` transfer the orbit between boxes and finally into one fixed class-dependent absorbing \`K_P\`. Distinguish **classwise common absorbing set**, **each scale's invariance**, and **history-dependent entry time**.

**DPT translation:** the interesting upgrade over \`stage_box_reachability.py\` is not another interval check but a **hybrid viability/guard-and-lift invariant set**. For physical stage domains \`K_s\`, define properly constrained flows within \`K_s\`, plus \`L_{s->t}(K_s ∩ guard_{s->t})⊂K_t\`. For a genuinely differentiable barrier \`b_s≤0\`, an inward Lie-derivative condition on the active boundary is a **separate classical hybrid-invariance proof** (compare [Maghenem–Sanfelice, Automatica 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2020.109328) or [sound invariant generation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10703-020-00355-z)). DPT's switched forced charge/thermal equations do not generally enjoy F149's weak-reversible, monomial-flux dominance. A box propagated through a few stages is not by itself an invariant or absorbing set. **Priority: conditional medium**, high only if genuine inward/guard certificates can be checked on the actual DPT branch.

Formalization caveat: the published [F149 Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/docs/149.md) explicitly covers the **September** boundedness/persistence manuscript, not this **October** uniform classwise absorption theorem. Do not label the latter Lean-verified by family association.

## F360: a proof architecture requiring distinctive curvature and topology

Original source: [body and appendix](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Global-Support-and-Convex-Injectivity-Domains-under-Weak-MTW-September-25-2026/build), [Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/docs/360.md).

1. The setting is a smooth compact boundaryless Riemannian manifold \`M\` (dimension at least 2), squared-distance cost \`c=d²/2\` and the weak MTW fourth-order curvature inequality **only for orthogonal direction pairs**. The theorem concerns convexity of tangent injectivity domains, allowing conjugate cut endpoints.
2. Represent the smooth branch-cost Hessian \`A_x(w)\` by the minimizing Jacobi-field index form. Its radial identity \`A_x(w)(w,·)=<w,·>\` and strict comparison of shortened geodesic durations supply a quantitative positive Hessian margin. Weak MTW supplies **transverse** Hessian concavity, not arbitrary convexity.
3. At a hypothetical first conjugate failure along a minimizing convex segment, a Jacobi kernel drives a negative trial-index estimate of order \`-const/(t²+1-r)\`. Orthogonal transverse concavity reduces the kernel to a segment direction, while the radial identity gives a competing lower bound of order \`-const/t\`; taking \`1-r=t²\` contradicts both.
4. For the subgradient projection \`F_t(x,p)=exp_x(tp)\`, the proof obtains local injectivity using finite active-log combinations, exactly cancels the *first-order* term before dividing by the squared separation, and retains a strictly positive transverse second-order margin. Invariance of domain plus a covering homotopic to the short-time homeomorphism upgrades local injectivity to global. The optional degree/area-formula appendix provides an alternate globalization route.
5. Global support is obtained by a **first-failing-time continuation**: show any \`t*<1\` would be nonconjugate and locally injective, hence a global homeomorphism and unique minimizer, excluding the supposed failure. Convexity of \`G_x\` and \`I(x)\` then follows by testing a two-support potential; it is a **consequence**, not a starting hypothesis.

**DPT translation:** its distinctive weak-MTW/Jacobi/covering proof is not available for an Eth DAE on evidence currently supplied. The useful **conditional strategy** is continuation of a known physical consistency branch:
\`R(y_plus,y_minus,theta)=0\`, with a validated nonzero smallest singular value of \`D_{y_plus}R\`, bounded \`(D_{y_plus}R)^{-1}D_theta R\` and no encounter with the branch/guard boundary. Under the usual compactness and implicit-function hypotheses, a selected local branch can continue in \`theta\`. This is **standard implicit continuation**, not a corollary of F360. It neither establishes that all roots coincide nor permits arbitrary event sequence changes. **Priority: WATCH / proof-pattern only** until a nontrivial DPT first-failure mechanism is identified.

Formalization scope in [lean/docs/360.md](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/lean/docs/360.md) covers the principal convex-injectivity conclusion and qualified finite-family results; it explicitly excludes some arbitrary-potential assertions in the manuscript.
