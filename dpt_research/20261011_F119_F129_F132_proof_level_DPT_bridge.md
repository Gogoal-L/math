## Scientific outcome before paper-by-paper detail

The three papers point to **three different obstructions** to a cheap and reliable DPT predictor, which must not be collapsed into one theorem:

- **F132 — grouped input effect:** a baseline one-coordinate perturbation may miss a response that is activated only by a *legal joint* change. Mixed finite contrasts and branch guards, not F132's Boolean exponent, are the DPT operational tool.
- **F129 — distinguishing context:** present-time observation equivalence can be broken by an admissible *future command word*. A guaranteed minimal dynamic state requires covering the declared command language, not a single scalar goal at one time; the paper's exponential lower bound is specific to a full relation alphabet and matching representation.
- **F119 — measurement-noise structure:** the best binary observable changes under correlated noise. Shared nuisance can make a contrast **exactly recoverable** even if single channels are unreliable. F119 proves coordinate optimality only in its iid binary-symmetric channel; its proof locally allows coupled child-pair laws, which is **not** a relaxation of iid channel noise.

These combine into a *legal context + grouped stimulus + nuisance-invariant readout* test. The strongest current artifact is a falsifiable, source-independent mathematical construction and explicit owner-facing DPT input contract—not a new engineering measurement claim.

## 1. F132: read the combinatorial proof, not just the headline

Source: [introduction and theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/A-superquadratic-separation-between-sensitivity-and-block-sensitivity-September-25-2026/build/sections/00-introduction.tex), [construction and TikZ target/gate figure](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/A-superquadratic-separation-between-sensitivity-and-block-sensitivity-September-25-2026/build/sections/01-construction.tex), [Proposition prop:recurrences](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/A-superquadratic-separation-between-sensitivity-and-block-sensitivity-September-25-2026/build/sections/02-recurrences.tex), [final separation and composition](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/A-superquadratic-separation-between-sensitivity-and-block-sensitivity-September-25-2026/build/sections/03-separation.tex). Inspected all four original section sources, including recurrence proof and the large-parameter cost.

**Exact object.** For a total Boolean f, s(f,x) counts one-bit flips changing f(x); bs(f,x) is the maximum number of **disjoint nonempty coordinate blocks** whose simultaneous flips change f(x). The paper builds total f with bs(f)>C s(f)^2 for every C, then a fixed exponent α>2 by composition. This is a statement about the **global maximum** of single-bit sensitivity, not just the derivative at one chosen input.

**Construction and proof hinges.** Fix depth d, M≥9^(d+1), L=d+2, k=2M²+1, r=ceil(sqrt M), t=16. A regular k-vertex tournament has k r child locations; each edge label selects a gate child. Parent clause i requires every one of its r target children to satisfy the strongest nested predicate; every outgoing edge i→j requires a *weaker* predicate of the selected child in row j to be zero. One flipped raw bit hits only one condition in any fixed clause, but may change **multiple** nested predicates in the same child. This is the reason the proof keeps two families, ordinary S_{level,0/1} and joint J_{level,0/1}, and does not merely reuse S.

Random labels guarantee no t-subset has compatible failed-target coordinates: union bound k^t r^(t-t(t-1)/2)≤3^16 M^(-20)<1. Thus fewer than 16 near-accepting target-only clauses exist. The induced tournament of gate-only near-accepting clauses has each outdegree≤1 and hence at most three vertices; the joint predicate J is required to count gate repairs. This gives the actual coupled recurrence (S and J at level ℓ, primed at ℓ-1):

    S1 <= M² S0' + r S1'      J1 <= M² J0' + r S1'
    S0 <= t S0' + S1' + 3J1'  J0 <= t S0' + 3J1'

Normalizing by M^(ℓ+b) and using ε=max(r,t)/M≤2/3^(d+1), the proof bounds S_{\ell,b}≤2L M^(\ell+b). The block construction gives M k^d disjoint zero-input sensitive blocks for each final predicate; OR-balancing across M copies yields global s(f)≤2L M^(d+1) and bs(f,0)≥M² k^d. Hence bs/s²≥2^d/[4(d+2)²]. At d=9, B/A²≥128/121; seed composition amplifies it.

**Feasibility counterweight.** The explicit d=9 seed requires M≥9^10=3,486,784,401, and the minimal prescribed ambient n=L M²(kr)^9 has **238 decimal digits**. This is an existence proof with huge dimensions; it supplies neither a feasible sensor design nor a physical bound on DPT information cost. The local toy d=1,M=3 (which does **not** meet the full sensitivity theorem's M bound) has 342 input bits, zero singleton positives at the all-zero baseline, but 57 disjoint six-bit positive blocks. This is an independent executable witness of the *missed local joint effect*, not a test of the theorem's global s(f) inequality.

**DPT test.** For two legally adjustable knobs a,b (or predeclared perturbations), evaluate the four *same-reference* future-goal readings G00,G10,G01,G11, with absolute scorer units and uncertainty. The interaction

    Δab G = G11-G10-G01+G00

can be large even if both baseline single-knob changes are zero, e.g. the explicit toy G(a,b)=ab. Physically admissible joint perturbation and command order must be qualified: simultaneous change of two circuit components is **not** equivalent to an arbitrary sequential word A;B. F132 does not prove a SiC charging, gate or inductance mechanism.

**Rejection gate.** If no physically legal pair of controls exists, or if standard second derivatives and the already available mixed_interaction_audit fully explain the measured nonlinearity, F132 contributes proof-taste and a negative-control check but **not** a new research route.

## 2. F129: why the exponent needs a much stronger alphabet than DPT has

Source: [model/theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/introduction.tex), [local matching encoding and candidate-slot diagrams](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/machines.tex), [rank/support corners](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/matching.tex), [rank-loss theorem and amplification figure](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/rank.tex), [context-separation lemma](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/separation.tex). Inspected these five original proof sections and their actual TikZ amplification and corner-reduction drawings.

**What is being reconstructed.** Each input letter is an arbitrary binary relation R on H=[h]; alphabet size is **2^(h²)**. Acceptance means R1...Rn is a nonempty relation. An h+3-state nondeterministic one-way automaton follows a surviving relation path. The deterministic two-way machine may revisit input, but has only s states; the proof encodes its successor graph into local Brauer perfect-matching diagrams on k=4(s+2)² ports (extra state handles the positive-transition convention). A sink component of the deterministic successor functional graph is a *tree*, whose cyclic tour yields a test-port matching. Candidate transition slots for every (direction,p,q) guarantee each cell's diagram depends **only** on that cell's own symbol.

**The critical proof passage.** Equality of two word diagrams implies equal acceptance in *all left/right contexts*. If relation matrices A and B differ at entry (i,j), the context

    Id_{i} A Id_{j}  vs  Id_{i} B Id_{j}

turns the differing entry into nonempty versus empty relation product. Since the alphabet contains *all* singleton identity matrices as single letters, the two words are distinguishable. Hence the generated matching submonoid surjects onto the **entire** relation monoid; this surjectivity (not a finite sample) is indispensable to the subsequent algebraic induction.

**Why 32→256 matters.** Pick 16 U vertices, 16 V vertices and a middle z. From 32 conjugates of one idempotent single-arrow lift obtain 16 additions (ui,z) and 16 additions (z,vj). Their products surrounded by P0=Id_{H\{z}} produce **256** distinct (ui,vj) additions. All matching lifts share support at most **64c**, where c is the original transversal-rank loss, because each idempotent support is bounded by twice its rank loss. Reducing at an idempotent preserves rank and a **single common support set**; repeated corners preserve the full relation alphabet on a smaller H of size h-31. Each of 256 steps must lose ≥L(h-31)/2 in rank, hence 64c≥128L(h-31), so c≥2L(h-31). Induction gives L(h)=2^floor((h-2)/31). The resulting two-way deterministic state bound is 4(s+2)²≥L(h). The figure's hub is a relation-algebra construction, not a physical voltage node or a claim of 256 DPT experiments.

The constant makes finite relevance very weak: to even exclude s=10 under the general bound requires h≥312, at which point the stipulated alphabet has 2^(312²)=2^97,344 letters. The deterministic code-check validates the h=2 syntactic-context lemma over every one of the 16 relation letters and every ordered pair, **not** this asymptotic lower-bound proof.

**DPT translation.** For source-reachable states h,h', present observation operator O, legal future command language W and goal G, define contextual goal distinguishability by existence of a *jointly legal* word w:

    ||O(h)-O(h')|| <= 2ε_obs   but   |G(F_w(h))-G(F_w(h'))| > 2τ_G.

Then an O-only deterministic τ_G predictor cannot cover both histories (with the specified compatible-error geometry). The selection of w must not depend on observing the forbidden future outcome. Left contexts correspond to **legal active preparation or calibration**, right contexts to available future commands/readouts. F129's arbitrary Id_i projectors generally cannot be implemented as SiC commands, and its exponential number of states **must not** be cited as a lower bound on DPT internal or hardware memory. Existing command_word_audit covers finite partial exact transition words; goal_lipschitz_stability handles finite-bank stable prediction under box noise. Neither is a theorem about native nonlinear all-word closure.

## 3. F119: seven links in the actual proof and a decisive correlated-noise counterexample

Source: [main definitions and sharp theorem](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/main.tex), [scalar curvature](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/scalar.tex), [canonical optimizer and reflected-core triangle figure](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/canonical.tex), [capacity](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/capacity.tex), [reserve](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/reserve.tex), [thinning](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/thinning.tex), [upgrade](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/upgrade.tex), [flow](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb/preprints/Sharp-binary-information-contraction-on-the-discrete-cube-September-24-2026/build/sections/flow.tex). Read the original main proof and all seven main supporting sections. Separate source-completeness from full independent certification of each analytic inequality.

**The exact channel.** X is uniform on {±1}^n; Z_i are independent of X,U **and of one another**, with E Z_i=ρ; Y_i=X_i Z_i. For u(x)=E[U|X=x]∈[-1,1], define L=ln 2, H(r) entropy of mean-r sign, ψ(r)=L-H(r), I(u)=H(Eu)-E H(u). The theorem states

    I(U;Y) <= ψ( |ρ| ψ^{-1}( I(U;X) ) ).

At fixed initial information, soft one-coordinate u(x)=a x_i attains equality. For a Boolean summary output with bias m=Eu, the bound refines to ψ(|ρ| ψ^{-1}(H(m))). Original text explicitly discusses contemporaneous Chen et al. Lemma 9.2 already implying the soft result; do not claim uniqueness/priority of the headline beyond the documented scope.

**Proof-dependency map (not the outline alone).**
1. Fourier/Walsh noise semigroup P_t=T_{e^{-t}}, generator N=½∑(Id-flip_i), and entropy production D(g)=E[atanh(g) Ng], yielding exact dI(P_tg)/dt=-D(P_tg).
2. Splitting one coordinate into g_+,g_- produces exact joining cost c(a,b)=(a-b)(atanh(a)-atanh(b))/4. The bare coordinate rate F(ψ(r))=r atanh(r) **does not** satisfy the local joining inequality for arbitrary coupled pair laws.
3. Define convex positive surplus S(I)=F(I)-2I and a **mean-dependent** reserve B_m(I)=2I+(1-m²)S(L-[H(m)-I]/(1-m²)). It pools the two children's entropy allocations through a convex perspective. Importantly the proof keeps each original child's individual entropy in the induction; only the **cost minimization** uses their average.
4. Prove canonical cost optimizer: a unique ordered interior pair (x,y) plus up to two equal-output corners (+1,+1) and (-1,-1). Lagrange pricing of a **single common entropy budget**, a convex positive series and affine dual supporting plane justify the restriction from arbitrary coupled pair laws to three atoms.
5. Prove capacity and thinning: the cost is linear in core mass; adding a common-output corner reduces the nonlinear information demand enough. The central strict analytic margin is 2-3M=2-4(ln2)²≈0.078187944327>0, where M=4(ln2)²/3. We independently checked with symbolic algebra the key polynomial identity and positivity decomposition in the thinning proof (both expanded differences **exactly zero**). The original figure is a barycentric triangle of **mixture weights**, and moving horizontally keeps core mass, mean separation, entropy and cost fixed while shifting the common mean to the one-corner wall; it is not a diagram of physical voltage/charge states.
6. The reserve already dominates F at I≥L/2. Below this threshold, the one-corner comparison and monotonicity of demand along the reflected-core path allow the hybrid profile max(B_m,F) to obey the local joining inequality. Induct on dimension to get D(g)≥F(I(g)).
7. With r_t=ψ^{-1}(I(P_tu)), derivative bound becomes r_t'≤-r_t, so r_t≤e^{-t}r_0; the theorem follows including endpoint and negative-ρ cases.

**Correlated-noise counterexample, derived and independently enumerated.** Keep fair independent X1,X2 but make Z1=Z2=Z with P(Z=1)=(1+ρ)/2. Then Y1Y2=X1X2 exactly, so I(X1X2;Y1,Y2)=ln2, while I(X1;Y1,Y2)=ψ(ρ). With **independent** Z_i instead, the latter is ψ(ρ) and the parity information is ψ(ρ²). At ρ=0.3: independent coordinate/parity = 0.065931944624509/0.005850828519107 bits; shared coordinate/parity = 0.065931944624509/1 bit. The original theorem is not contradicted because the iid-noise hypothesis was changed. The original optimizer permits *coupling of the two face fields inside the cost relaxation*; that is a completely different dependence from shared sensor noise.

**DPT translation and hard boundary.** The correct positive structure is **goal-aligned shared-nuisance cancellation**, not "parity is optimal for SiC". In a local deterministic linear observation model y=Mx+Nη, an exact scalar goal c^T x has a nuisance-invariant linear readout w^T y whenever M^T w=c and N^T w=0 (in the legally allowed state tangent). Example M=I_2, N=(1,1)^T, c=(1,-1)^T, w=(1,-1)^T: common offset cancels exactly. By contrast c=(1,1)^T has no such invariant readout; x=(1,1),η=-1 and x=(-1,-1),η=+1 both give y=0 and goal difference 4, requiring worst-case absolute prediction error≥2 on that bounded class. This is elementary linear algebra, not a direct consequence of F119. It motivates frozen paired/readout contrasts or CAL measurements only when they share demonstrably identical nuisance under valid physical ports and noise/correlation budgets. Shared gain, drift, skew and filtering are **not** interchangeable without measurement-chain evidence.

## 4. A minimal integrated proof-of-concept joining the three mechanisms

Define a **symbolic** reachable-state history h∈{-1,+1}, two legal finite actions A and B which set action flags a,b from zero to one, and future target g(h,a,b)=h·a·b. At initial flags (0,0), applying A alone or B alone yields goal 0 regardless of h; applying the ordered word AB yields g=h. A pretarget observation y0=η contains only a shared nuisance, so states h=±1 are observation-aliasing yet have AB-goal gap 2; every y0-only minimax scalar predictor has worst-case error **at least 1** on this two-member class. This combines the **joint-effect** motif (F132) with **future-word distinguishability** (F129), without borrowing either theorem's exponent.

A legal *before-target* active calibration might return y1=h+η along with y0=η. Then y1-y0=h and AB-goal prediction becomes exact in this toy, conditional on truly common η. If the two observations instead carry different nuisance values η1,η0, the readout error is η1-η0 and exact recovery is lost. This is the **correlation-structure** motif illustrated sharply by F119's separate binary-channel counterexample; it is not implied by F119 for analog DPT.

No numerical values in this toy refer to C3M0075120K, LTspice, the Pro18 actual-law state, or a physical transistor. It is deliberately falsifiable in a source-complete implementation.

## 8. Constructive synthesis: exact finite-command target quotient with unconstrained shared nuisance

This result is **elementary independent linear algebra**, not a direct corollary of any of the three OpenAI theorems. It provides a truly executable bridge between the F129 contextual distinguishability viewpoint and F119's lesson on nuisance correlation.

**Proposition (finite-word prediction-time observability).** Let x∈R^n be a state on one common linear chart, let future commands in a specified finite list W apply fixed matrices A_u, let goals be rows of C∈R^{q×n}, and let prediction-time measurements be y=H x+Nη, with η∈R^p arbitrary and unbounded (unknown). For each word w=(u1,...,uℓ), F_w=A_{uℓ}...A_{u1}; stack all C F_w rows as G_W. Take U_0 with orthonormal columns spanning ker(N.T) and define R=U_0.T H.

1. Every listed future goal is uniquely determined by y for *all possible x,η* if and only if row(G_W)⊆row(R).
2. If arbitrary ideal new scalar measurements of x without new nuisance may be added, the minimum number needed for simultaneous exact recovery of the listed future goals equals
   \[
   k_{\min}=\operatorname{rank}\begin{bmatrix}R\\G_W\end{bmatrix}-\operatorname{rank}R.
   \]
3. For any single goal row g outside row(R), let v be the orthogonal projection of g.T onto ker(R), normalized. Then two histories x=±v are observationally compatible for some (possibly different) nuisance assignments and have future goal gap 2|gv|>0. If ||x||<=1 is the accepted symmetric prior and all such states and nuisance offsets are admissible, every decoder has worst-case error ≥|gv|; for unbounded x the ambiguity can be scaled without limit.

**Proof.** Two x states have observationally compatible images precisely when Hδx∈range(N), equivalent to U_0.T H δx=Rδx=0. A goal is constant on these observation fibers iff ker(R)⊆ker(G_W), equivalent by finite-dimensional row/null duality to row(G_W)⊆row(R). Arbitrary appended noiseless linear rows can expand R by at most one independent dimension per feature, giving the lower bound. A basis of a complement of row(R) in row(R)+row(G_W) attains it, giving sufficiency. A witness along the projected g component gives the opposite-state lower bound, with equal recorded y obtained by adjusting η. These are identities for an **unconstrained common linear chart**, not for arbitrary DPT-reachable trajectories.

**Decisive two-state example.** H=I2, N=[1,1].T; instantaneous goal C=[1,-1]. Its value is recovered exactly by the nuisance-invariant contrast y1-y2. One future legal abstract command A=diag(1,-1) changes the target row to CA=[1,1]. This row is not in row([1,-1]), so a one-dimensional observation-only prediction defect appears despite instantaneous sufficiency. A new *ideal* reading of x1+x2 eliminates the deficit, and so does a distinct legal acquisition measuring η itself through a third sensor row (H_extra=[0,0], N_extra=[1]), which changes the nuisance knowledge rather than directly sensing state.

**Mandatory DPT qualification:** A is a global linear map only in this toy. DPT 18B/18C actual-law Jacobians change with preparation, command, branch/guard and thermal memory, and θ-space may have nonlinear curvature. Source gradient and observations must share the same units, exact ports, timing, state lift and achieved conditions; numerical near-zero singular values require a physically justified rank tolerance and nuisance floor. A previously recorded common offset need not equal a later calibration offset. Real source-reachable history sets may not contain opposite witnesses, and a finite command list does not cover all futures. A finite k_min never proves sensor count, physical model reduction or unbounded error-free reconstruction on actual hardware.
